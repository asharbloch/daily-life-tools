Free Online Calculators for School Budgets

Planning a school budget is easier when you can see the numbers clearly. Start by listing the regular costs for the term, such as textbooks, stationery, transport, lunch, and activities, then add them up and compare the total with the money available. A quick calculation helps families spot where spending can be trimmed, set aside a small amount for unexpected costs, and keep school expenses under control through the year.

To work through the figures for your own household, try a free online calculator for school budgets and test different totals before you commit to a spending plan.

Unstitched Lawn Suits for Everyday Wear

Unstitched lawn is a practical choice for everyday wear in warm weather. The light, breathable fabric is comfortable through a long day, and buying it unstitched means the outfit can be tailored to fit well and suit personal taste. Choose a print and colour that works for daily use, pair it with a simple dupatta or trousers, and keep the stitching neat and comfortable for regular wear.
